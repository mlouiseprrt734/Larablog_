<h1>Larablog Project</h1>
<h2>Presentation</h2>
<p>This is a blog made in Laravel Framework. It allows you to have access to multiple articles written by many authors.</p>

<h2>Fonctionnalities</h2>
<p>The fonctionnalities differ wether you are a guest or connected with your account.</p>

<h3>Guest</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Home page with insight of 4 of the most liked articles</li>
    <li>Access to the About and Policy Privacy pages</li>
    <li>Access to the Register and Login forms</li>
</ul>

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f786a728-2084-4bfa-8036-b084d26158f2)


<h3>Connected</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Dashboard with your articles</li>
    <li>Add articles<li>
    <li>Manage your own articles (edit and delete)</li>
    <li>Access to all the articles (filter by author available)</li>
    <li>Access to the details of each article and possibility to comment and like</li>
    <li>Likes and comments are restricted to 5 by minutes</li>
    <li>Profile management and Logout</li>
</ul>
